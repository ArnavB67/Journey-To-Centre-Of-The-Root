Journey To Centre Of Root

This is a multiplayer action platformer game based on webrtc peer to peer connection. I used Tubes addon to support multiplayer sessions and hence players can connect via web rtc without need of an external server.

No Web Build Since Multiplayer Didnt Work on Web, Hence Please Download the exe to run the game, while running dont run the console, just the normal exe

Players Spawn Near a computer and they can choose between 3 characters to Play by interacting with the computer by pressing the key 'E' when they get near it.

Characters available are :

1) Warrior : Warrior is a melee Based character which has a passive ability to double jump and has a fast run speed, the primary attack binded on the keybind C is a slash attack giving a 10 HP damage, it has a cooldown of 1 second, a secondary more powerful attack giving a 25 HP damage binded on keybind X is a smash Attack Which has a cooldown of 5 seconds.
If The Health of Warrior Gets Below 25 He can Perform a Sacrificial move, by pressing 'Q' the Warrior Sacrifices himself and moves towards the nearest enemy and explodes and gives a damage of 80 HP.

2)Wizard : Wizard Is a Mage, he has a slow speed and low jump velocity naturally, but has a great passive ability of Regeneration. The Wizard has its primary attack Buff binded to the keybind 'C' which gives the Wizard a buff increasing his movement speed and jump velocity, it has a cooldown of 10 seconds and the buff lasts for 5 seconds. While the buff is active the cooldown for Secondary Attack Spell(AttackBall) is also reduced to 2 seconds instead of the regular 3 seconds. The Secondary Attack Spell fires a AttackBall in the direction Aimed, we aim with the help of mouse cursor.
If The Health of Wizard Gets Below 25 he can also perform a sacrificial move by pressing 'Q'. The Wizard Sacrifices his life and revives a Dead Player to full HP at the location where the Player Died

3)Archer : Archer has a higher base jump compared to other Characters and movement speed slightly lesser than Warrior but greater than Wizard.The Archer has a higher FOV as a passive ability. For the primary Attack it fires an Arrow in the aimed direction giving a 10 HP damage to the damaged enemy, it has a cooldown of a second. The Archer has a secondary Attack Which produces a path made of rope to the fired point on ground, players can walk on it, and the path lasts for 8 seconds, the cooldown for this secondary attack is 5 seconds.
When The Health of Archer Drops below 25 He can fire Poisonous Arrows in Aimed direction by pressing Q,The poison gives a damage of 5 HP every 2 seconds to the boss enemy.

Once Everyone Selects their Character their goal is to follow the path defeat enemies , reach the boss room and defeat him.

The Game has various features which includes:

1)it has multiplayer where you can host a server and people can join it with a given code with a username

2)There are particle effects for effects and some action

3)Dead Players can spectate alive players and cycle through them by pressing left mouse button.

4)Map Contains Traps and laser Beams

The Game Has 2 Types of Enemies : 
1) A Mushroom Enemy : It has only one attack that is a melee attack, after it attacks the player it gets stunned for a while and then agains move towards the player to attack, the mushroom enemy can jump across platforms to chase Players
2) There is also a boss fight, the boss has high movement speed and jump velocity it has a random attack style, the boss either gets close to make a melee attack or chooses to fire off a spell or sometimes if player gets too close it can decide to retreat backwards and fire off a spell, sometimes decide to rapid fire a spell.

The Controls are :

1)A,D for left and right movement

2)Space Bar For Jump

3)C for Primary Attack

4)X for Secondary Attack

5)Q for Low Health Attack
